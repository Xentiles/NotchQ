# Code of Conduct

NotchQ's issues and discussions should be a friendly, useful place for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep to the topic: NotchQ and how it works on people's Macs.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Protect privacy, including your own. Don't post credentials, private conversations or personal details.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Sexualised content, threats, or encouraging harm.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, trolling, or repeatedly derailing a conversation.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's built-in tools. Open the **⋯** menu on the comment or issue, choose **Report content**, then pick **Report to repository admins** for the maintainer, or **Report abuse** for GitHub. Reports to repository admins are only visible to the maintainer.

## What happens

The maintainer reviews every report and may edit, hide or remove content, lock conversations, or block people who break this code, in proportion to what happened. Decisions consider the whole context. This code applies to all of NotchQ's spaces on GitHub.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
